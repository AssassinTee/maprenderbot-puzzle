# Noita

## Hint

Opening this map reveals a screenshot from the unsolved ["Eye Messages"](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Eye_Messages) puzzle from the game Noita

## Solution

The Eye Messages are not part of the puzzle but a meta joke about unsolved puzzles, which are taking a huge community effort to solve.
The screenshot also contains position coordinates in the top right which are used at a later step
