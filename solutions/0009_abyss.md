# Abyss

## Hint

Rendering the TV in Abyss reveals, that the DDNet client is opened (instead of credits) and shows the ddnet map editor

## Solution

If you look closely, you can see that 2 maps are opened in the editor. One is named `Noita` and one is named `xQc`.

Next you render the maps with:

/maprender name:Noita
/maprender name:xQc
